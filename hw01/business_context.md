## Which metric of credit score, debt-to-income ratio, annual income is the most reliable when predicting when a person will default on a loan?
- Credit score is the most reliable indicator when predicting when a person will default. The other two indicators, DTI and annual income, cannot predict default rates as well. It is not worth including the other two indicators with credit score when predicting the default rate.


## What credit risks might apply to education that might not apply to the other areas?
- Eduction loans typically funds assets that do not exist yet. The other areas usually spend the loan on assets that can be liquidated. An example of these assets would be the diploma that the student recieves with the help of a loan.

## What is the difference between delinquency and defaulting and how they are mapped onto the four status categories?
- Delinquency refers to when a borrower missing one or more payments. Defaulting on a loan refers to when a loan repayment is not possible with the agreed upon terms. Current and paid-off are positive categories to be in, while delinquency and defaulting are negative.
